# Introduction

Yellow Card's API gives you access to Africa and emerging markets' most comprehensive digital asset and fiat payment infrastructure. Whether you're building a remittance platform, enabling crypto transactions, or managing multi-currency treasury - our API handles the complexity of moving money across borders and between fiat and digital currencies.

This documentation will guide you from your first API call to production-ready integration.

# What you can build with Yellow Card:

  


## Digital Asset infrastructure

![](https://files.readme.io/1c5f492138fdf2258e7c9720eeec9531fb906ec27f5f092da90a2355ce247b66-image.png)   


**Send & Receive Stablecoins**  
Send and receive Stablecoins with any wallet globally. Move assets instantly across 30+ blockchains (Ethereum, Tron, Solana, and more). 

**Custodial Wallets & Custom Wallets** Create wallets for internal Stablecoin management or enable customer subwallets (Custom wallets) with flexible options. We handle all blockchain and Stablecoin layers.

**Embedded Compliance** No need to worry about integrating 3+ different compliance and fraud vendors. Our API has them integrated by default so you don't have to.

  


## Fiat Payment infrastructure

![](https://files.readme.io/54be108324546a2c52c5e528bce1db9ec3b44f0fdcc550078a19a80301b4b69a-image.png)   


**Send & Receive 50+ Currencies** Convert to and from USDT, USDC, and other Stablecoins with USD, NGN, KES, and 50+ major and local currencies.

**Global USD Payments & Invoice Settlement** Make payments and settle invoices globally in USD, EUR, and local currencies in nearly 200 countries. 

**Local Fiat Wallets & Custom Wallets** Create your own wallets to manage local currency balances (KES, NGN, etc.), or create subwallets (Custom wallets) for local currency acceptance from your customers (custom fiat wallets coming soon).

**Virtual USD/EUR accounts** (Coming soon) Create and manage unique USD and EUR accounts with dedicated IBANs for your business and your customers with full on/off ramp support.
