## Overview

Enable your customers to send and receive USD or EUR - regardless of where they are in the world. Yellow Card's USD/EUR access allows your business to accept local currency from senders, convert it to USD stablecoins, and deliver it as USD/EUR to recipients globally. You can also receive USD/EUR directly from customers into your USD/EUR accounts.

  


### How it Works:

**Send USD/EUR:** Customer pays in local currency (e.g., KES) → Converts to USD Stablecoin → Sends USD/EUR out to recipient

**Receive USD/EUR:** Customer sends USD/EUR → Your USD/EUR account is topped up

  


### Who This is For

  * **Remittance platforms** enabling cross-border money transfers
  * **Freelance payment platforms** paying workers in USD/EUR
  * **International marketplaces** facilitating global transactions
  * **Payroll services** disbursing salaries in stable currencies
  * **E-commerce platforms** accepting and sending USD/EUR payments
